🎙️ TTS Pro User Guide

Welcome to TTS Pro, your Material You Text-to-Speech companion.

🚀 Quick Start
1. Enter your text in the "Message" box.
2. Select your target language from the top bar.
3. Tap "Speak" to hear it immediately.
4. Tap "Translate & Speak" to convert English text to your selected language before playing.

💾 Saving Audio
- Tap the Save icon (diskette) to export your speech as a .wav file.
- Files are saved to your "Music/TTSPro" folder.

⚙️ Settings (Left Drawer)
- Open the menu (☰) to adjust:
  * Voice Pitch: Higher for squeaky, lower for deep voices.
  * Reading Speed: Adjust how fast the voice speaks.
  * Permissions: Grant Microphone access for upcoming features.

🌗 Themes
- Tap the Sun/Moon icon in the top right to toggle Dark Mode.
- On Android 12+, the app automatically matches your wallpaper colors!

🌐 Supported Languages
English, Arabic, Bengali, Hindi, Urdu, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Chinese, Japanese, Portuguese, and Russian.
